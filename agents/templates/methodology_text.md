# Methodology & Disclaimer Text — The Oversight Report
## From Paski (March 2026)

---

## Lawyer-Facing (for attorney PDF reports)

Methodology and Verification: The Oversight Report compiles findings from structured public CMS datasets and selected state sources, then normalizes and cross-references those records into a facility-level evidence file with source dates, versioning, and citations. Attorney-facing reports are generated from a frozen evidence bundle and reviewed through a verification workflow that checks data freshness, numeric consistency, citation support, benchmark validity, and language safety before release. Reports that contain unresolved inconsistencies, low-confidence joins, or unsupported claims are held for human review rather than delivered automatically. This approach is designed to make each report traceable, contestable, and defensible.

---

## Consumer-Friendly (for family reports / website)

How we build and check reports: The Oversight Report uses public CMS and state data to create a clear picture of each nursing home's staffing, inspections, penalties, ownership, and quality patterns. Before a report is delivered, we check that the numbers are current, the sources are cited, and the language matches what the data actually supports. Reports include data dates and limitations so readers can understand what the findings do—and do not—show. This process is meant to make reports more transparent, trustworthy, and easier to question or verify.

## Website Footer / Short Disclaimer

Data methodology: Reports are built from public CMS and state datasets, checked for source dates, numeric consistency, and citation support, and may undergo human review before release. Findings are intended to be transparent, traceable, and contestable—not a substitute for legal, medical, or regulatory judgment.

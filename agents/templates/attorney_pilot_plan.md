# The Oversight Report — Attorney Pilot Plan
## From Paski (March 2026)

---

## 1. Pilot Goal

Validate that attorneys will pay for a clinician-reviewed, evidence-backed nursing home report that helps with:
- Case screening
- Early factual development
- Demand letter support

## 2. Pilot Size

- 5 attorneys max
- 1–3 reports each
- 5–10 total reports
- Do NOT go bigger yet

## 3. Offer

A manually reviewed Evidence Report built from public CMS and state data, with citations, data dates, and clinician oversight.

## 4. Pricing

- Single pilot report: $99
- 3-report pilot pack: $249
- Keep pricing low enough to learn, not to maximize margin yet

## 5. Included

- 1 PDF report per facility
- Staffing, penalties, deficiencies, ownership, quality signals
- Citations and data dates
- Manual clinician review before delivery
- One short email follow-up for clarification

## 6. NOT Included

- Legal advice
- Expert opinion
- Guarantee of claim strength
- Medical causation analysis
- Custom litigation strategy

## 7. Workflow

1. Attorney sends facility name/CCN
2. Generate frozen evidence bundle
3. Report draft generated
4. Guardian verification checks
5. Router sends to human review
6. Review with checklist
7. Deliver PDF
8. Ask for feedback

## 8. Success Metrics

Track for every pilot:
- Did they pay?
- Turnaround time
- Did they say it saved time?
- Did they request another report?
- Did they find errors?
- Would they use it again?
- Would they refer another attorney?

## 9. Success Threshold

Pilot is successful if:
- 3+ paid reports sold
- At least 2 attorneys say it was useful
- Correction rate is low
- At least 1 repeat request

## 10. Intake Form

Ask only:
- Attorney / firm name
- Email / phone
- Facility name
- State
- CCN if known
- Purpose: screening / demand / investigation
- What do you most want to understand?
- Any known incident dates or focus areas
- Deadline

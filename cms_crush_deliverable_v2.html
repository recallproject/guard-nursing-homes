⚠ Critical Methodology Note — Signal vs. Noise
The raw 2.27M number includes legitimate large health systems (Mayo Clinic, Stanford, MGH) where thousands of providers are correctly registered at one campus address. The fraud signal lives in mid-range clusters (4–50 NPIs) at non-hospital addresses — office suites, strip malls, and registered agent locations. Our pipeline identifies these; the clinical plausibility layer separates real fraud from legitimate group practices. Similarly, the 76,389 name-matched LEIE hits include false positives from common names (e.g., "David Brown" in multiple states). The 1,670 NPI-matched hits are high-confidence — same NPI number on both the active registry and the exclusion list.

  • Red Flag: Diagnostic clinic at same address as excluded ophthalmologist — possible referral loop
  • Suspicious: 26 NPIs at one address with 3 already excluded — what about the other 23?

📍 Address: [1629 K ST NW STE 300, Washington, DC]

  • 205 NPIs registered at this single suite
  • 3 LEIE-excluded providers still registered here
  • 12 NPIs sharing the same phone number
  • 47 NPIs registered as DMEPOS suppliers
  • Taxonomy codes span 14 different specialties

🏥 Clinical Plausibility Assessment

  • Impossible: 205 independent medical practices cannot operate from a single office suite
  • Impossible: 14 specialties at one address with no hospital affiliation
  • Suspicious: DMEPOS supplier count exceeds physical capacity
  • Suspicious: Excluded providers maintaining active registrations

Phone Number Clustering — Shell Company Signals

When 3+ NPIs at fraud ring addresses share a phone number, it strongly suggests coordinated operations under a single owner — a classic DMEPOS shell company pattern.

How Phone Clustering Works

SignalWhat It MeansExample
3–5 NPIs Possible group practice — needs review Small clinic with multiple providers
6–15 NPIs Likely coordinated — flagged for investigation Management company or nominee structure

Rob Benard is a licensed NP (Acute Care + Psychiatry) who has worked bedside in the facilities we're analyzing. When we say "205 providers can't practice from a single suite," that's a clinically defensible statement — not a statistical guess.

Every dataset used is publicly available from CMS and OIG. No proprietary claims data, no vendor lock-in, no black box. CMS can reproduce every finding independently. This is exactly the "Explainable AI" the RFI requested.

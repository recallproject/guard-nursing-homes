Federal Staffing Requirements

Understanding the regulatory framework is critical for interpreting staffing data.

Current Law (42 CFR §483.35)

An RN on site 8+ consecutive hours/day, 7 days/week. A full-time RN as DON. "Sufficient" licensed nursing staff 24/7.

Key gap: No federal minimum HPRD requirement exists. The "sufficient" standard is qualitative, not quantitative.

Timeline: The 2024 Staffing Rule

May 2024
CMS finalizes first-ever minimum staffing rule: 3.48 total HPRD + 24/7 RN coverage
April–June 2025
Federal courts in Texas and Iowa strike down the rule before it takes effect
July 4, 2025
One Big Beautiful Bill Act (§71111) blocks enforcement through September 2034
December 2, 2025
CMS formally repeals the 2024 standards (effective February 2, 2026)
February 2, 2026
18 state AGs (led by CA, MA, NY) request targeted 3.48 HPRD for high-risk operators
Critical fact: The 24/7 RN requirement never took effect at any facility.

Government Data Transparency Changes

When CMS removes data from public view, we document it here.

CMS removed complaint counts from Care Compare. We reconstructed them from federal inspection records — each record flags whether it was complaint-triggered. This is a direct count from the same records CMS previously used.

CMS suspended enhanced ownership disclosure requirements from the 2024 package. We continue reporting from the currently available CMS Ownership file. Some proposed enhancements (PE/REIT identification) may not be reflected.

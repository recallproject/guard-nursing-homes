The Oversight Report

Research a nursing home before you choose

OversightReports helps families look up Medicare-certified nursing homes using the same federal inspection, staffing, and ownership records CMS publishes — free, with no login and no paywall.

It is built for people making difficult care decisions: adult children, spouses, and caregivers who need plain-English safety context before a placement, transfer, or tour.

Each facility report can include CMS star ratings, nurse staffing hours, health-inspection deficiencies, immediate-jeopardy and serious harm flags, federal fines and payment denials, and ownership or chain affiliations when those fields appear in public data.

All figures come from public government / CMS sources such as Care Compare, Payroll-Based Journal staffing, penalty files, and ownership disclosures. We compile and explain the record; we do not replace a tour, a clinician, or CMS.gov.
